Here's the loop, using a concrete example: **you coach Sam**, who trains on his own phone.

## 1. Sam sends you a snapshot

Sam opens **Settings → "Coaching a friend"** and taps **"Share my training with a coach"**. The app builds a code from his own state — profile name, his unit, his routines with per-set targets, and his last 24 sessions — and offers **Copy link** / **Copy code**. He texts it to you.

What travels: routine names, exercise names, set counts, per-set reps or seconds, loads, and what he actually did last time (including sets he missed). What never travels: his AI key, his programs, his own coach suggestions, his body metrics.

## 2. You read it

You tap **"Coaching a friend"** in your Coach tab hero (or **Settings → Open coaching**). That's a separate overlay with its own roster — it does not swap or reload your own training data.

- **"Add an athlete from a code"** → paste → **Read share** → "Added Sam".
- His card shows **routines** (collapsed, e.g. *Push A — 5 exercises · 16 sets*, expanding to `Ring Dip — 8/8/8 reps @ 10 kg`) and **recent sessions** with dates and how many sets were completed.

Opening the link on a phone does the same thing: the link is just `…/#perseus-coach=…`, so it lands straight on the paste-and-read screen, prefilled.

## 3. You set next targets

**"Set next targets"** opens the editor for one routine at a time. For each movement you get one input per set he already does, plus load, plus quick buttons: **+1 rep**, **+2.5 kg**, **Match last**. Under each name: *"3 sets · last: 8 · 8 · 7 @ 10 kg · 12 Nov"*.

You write `9 / 9 / 8 @ 10 kg` on the dips, add a note — *"3rd set stop one rep shy"* — and tap **Send to athlete**. Out comes a `#perseus-plan=…` link or code. If you're not finished, **Save draft** keeps your edits per athlete.

**The set count is not yours to change.** The editor only gives you as many inputs as he already does, and applying never adds or removes a set — you tune reps, seconds and load, which is what progressive overload needs.

## 4. Sam applies it

Sam goes to **Settings → "Apply a plan from my coach"** (or just taps your link) and pastes. **Preview changes** shows the diff before anything is written:

```
Ring Dip     8/8/8 @ 10 kg  →  9/9/8 @ 10 kg
Ring Push-up 12 reps        →  12 reps (unchanged)
```

with your note underneath. He confirms; only then does his routine change, all-or-nothing, through the normal save path. Then he logs his next session as usual — the new targets are just his numbers now, and his own logging, suggestions and coach tab behave exactly as before.

## 5. Next week

Sam sends a fresh snapshot (same two taps), which replaces his record in your roster — name-based, so it doesn't duplicate him. You see the 9/9/8 was hit, so you go to 12.5 kg. Repeat.

## The rules that keep it harmless

- **Nothing syncs automatically.** Each share is a snapshot; you only see what he last sent, and he only changes what he applies. No server, no accounts.
- **Your own data is separate.** Your roster lives in its own browser key; reading or editing Sam never rewrites your routines, sessions or pending suggestions. The only time a plan touches `perseus-v1` is when *you* apply a plan on *your* device.
- **Matching is by name** — routine name, then exercise name. Rename either side and that movement is reported as skipped rather than guessed wrong.
- **One plan covers one routine** — pick another routine from the selector to send a second link.
- **Units**: he keeps his unit; your loads are converted when they land if his device is set differently.
